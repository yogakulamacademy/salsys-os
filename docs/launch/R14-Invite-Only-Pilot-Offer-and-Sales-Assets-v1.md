## 1. Proposed initial buyer

Owner-led education, training and wellness businesses that already receive regular enquiries and have a small sales/admissions team. This is the **proposed** starting vertical, not a customer segment approved for paid advertising or outreach.

### Buying problem

- New enquiries arrive through different channels, but ownership and next actions become unclear.
- Staff lose context between conversations, tasks and follow-ups.
- Business owners struggle to see which leads need attention today.
- Reporting should distinguish genuine evidence from missing or inferred data.

### What's included in the proposed *base* pilot

- A guided walkthrough and scoped manual onboarding process
- Lead workspace and pipeline
- Assigned work and follow-up task management
- Customer context/timeline where legitimate source evidence exists
- Human-led support boundaries to be agreed in writing

**Not included or not promised:** autonomous AI replies, unattended WhatsApp/Instagram campaigns, behavioral tracking activation, all third-party integrations, guaranteed campaign attribution, verified increase in revenue, self-service trials, automated SaaS billing, unlimited usage or round-the-clock support.

### How the pilot works

1. **Discuss your current workflow.** We learn how leads arrive and where follow-ups are missed.
2. **See a real product demonstration.** Any example data is clearly marked as demonstration data.
3. **Agree on a written pilot scope.** Features, users, term, price, support and privacy obligations must be explicitly approved.
4. **Complete access and privacy acceptance.** Real customer records are not imported until the necessary tenant isolation, permissions and operational checks pass.
5. **Begin a controlled pilot.** A platform administrator provisions a workspace only after the required approvals.

### FAQ

**Can SalsysOS replace every tool our team uses?**  
Not automatically. The pilot starts with specific CRM workflows. Integrations are evaluated separately and are included only when technically verified and authorized.

**Does the pilot include AI sending automatic replies?**  
No. Autonomous customer messaging is outside the baseline pilot until its independent safeguards and acceptance requirements are met.

**Can we connect WhatsApp, Instagram or our website?**  
That depends on each channel's permissions, integration state, consent requirements and a separately approved scope. We will not promise an unverified connection.

**Will you import our real customer data during the demo?**  
No. External demos must use legitimately approved, sanitized data. Live data activation requires security and privacy acceptance.

**How much does the pilot cost?**  
The exact fee, currency, period and cancellation terms will be stated in a written, owner-approved offer. No public price is approved yet.

## 3. Discovery call — eight questions

1. Where do your leads come from today: website, calls, WhatsApp, Instagram or referrals?
2. Approximately how many new enquiries arrive per week or month?
3. Who takes responsibility for each new lead?
4. What usually causes a missed follow-up?
5. Where is the history of conversations and decisions recorded?
6. How do you know when a lead becomes qualified or ready to buy?
7. What would make this pilot demonstrably useful after its agreed period?
8. Which team members would need access, and who can approve data use, contract terms and payment?

**Qualification standard:** A qualified prospect has a real problem, named decision-maker, scoped intended workflow and willingness to review written terms. No inferred buying score or synthetic revenue claim.
