Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

The proposed Free tier costs ₹0 per month and does not require paid setup. Public self-service signup has not been released yet.

Are these monthly prices per user?

No. They are proposed prices per business workspace, with provisional named-user and lead-record limits.

Is WhatsApp or Instagram included automatically?

No. Eligibility does not automatically turn on a provider integration. Each connection depends on permissions, source consent, technical checks and a separately agreed scope.

Does Pro include autonomous AI replies?

No. Autonomous messaging is not part of these offers until separately reviewed, approved and released.

What happens after the managed pilot?

A customer can choose an agreed plan or separately negotiated managed service. No automatic ₹7,999 continuation, renewal or charge is offered.

Can I buy or create an account today?

Self-service activation and subscription checkout remain unreleased. This page is a review-only pricing preview and does not collect contact or payment information.
