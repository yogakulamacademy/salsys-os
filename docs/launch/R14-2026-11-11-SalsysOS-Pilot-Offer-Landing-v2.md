## A. Positioning and target customer

**Product:** SalsysOS — Sales + Analysis Operating System.
**Initial sub-offer:** SalsysOS Managed CRM Pilot.
**Core promise:** *Stop losing track of enquiries. Give every lead an owner, clear stage, and next follow-up.*
**What not to promise:** automatic revenue growth, all integrations ready, trained predictive accuracy, automatic WhatsApp or Instagram replies, comprehensive behavioral attribution, or an independently verified privacy/security certification.

**First vertical (proposed):** India-based owner-led education, training, coaching and wellness businesses selling courses/programmes/services that require repeated follow-up.

**Ideal initial prospect (qualification heuristics, not software limits):**
- Approximately 100–1,000 incoming enquiries/month, from website, social, phone, messaging or referrals.
- 2–8 sales/admissions staff; first pilot includes only three named users.
- A higher-consideration sale and recurring handoffs; leads routinely need multiple follow-ups.
- A specific owner/decision-maker, existing lawful prospect contacts, a willingness to work with approved demo data and a realistic process-improvement success measure.
- No requirement that unapproved WhatsApp/Instagram/marketing tracking integrations be activated on day one.

**Not first-market:** one-person freelancers needing a free basic CRM; enterprises requiring complex compliance/integration guarantees; regulated health/financial data workflows; real estate workflow vertical (R12 remains deferred); customers who cannot use CRM without a blocked provider connector.

### Problem block — When leads come in, work should not fall through

Enquiries arrive from many places. Ownership changes. Conversations continue. Important follow-ups are missed because context lives across separate messages, spreadsheets and people's memories.

SalsysOS gives your team one place to track the operational next step.

### Benefit 1 — Every lead has an owner

See who is responsible, where an enquiry stands and what should happen next. Keep management and team members aligned with appropriate role-based access.

### Benefit 2 — Follow-ups become visible work

Keep upcoming and overdue tasks in view, move enquiries through the pipeline and avoid relying entirely on manual reminders.

### Benefit 3 — Customer context with evidence

Review available conversation and journey context where connected records are legitimately sourced. Unverified events or missing integrations are not presented as certain facts.

### What's in the 60-day managed pilot?

- One business workspace for up to three named users.
- Leads, Pipeline, Tasks and Follow-ups.
- Lead ownership and appropriately scoped team access.
- Available Customer 360 context where legitimate source evidence exists.
- Two hours of guided setup and one hour of team orientation.
- Two short progress-review calls during the pilot.

**Pilot recommendation (subject to commercial sign-off):** INR 14,999 for 60 days, plus applicable taxes. No automatic renewal. If approved for publication, show exactly this with a clear “Request a walkthrough” CTA, not a misleading “Start free” button.

**Not in the base pilot:** autonomous AI/WhatsApp/Instagram messages, optional behavior tracking, unverified provider connectors, and guaranteed conversion/revenue results. Additional integrations are evaluated and approved separately.

### How it works

1. **Tell us what happens to your enquiries today.** We identify the biggest follow-up or handoff gap.
2. **See a guided SalsysOS walkthrough.** We demonstrate verified product workflows with approved demo records.
3. **Agree on a written pilot.** Confirm scope, price, users, support, privacy, and real success criteria.
4. **Verify safe access.** Confirm tenant separation, role access, data handling and operational readiness before using real records.
5. **Activate only when ready.** An authorized admin provisions the scoped workspace. The 60-day period starts at successful activation.

### FAQ

**Is SalsysOS a free CRM?**
This is a guided, paid pilot proposal—not a free self-service plan. Any commercial offer is confirmed in writing before payment.

**Is the pilot designed for our business?**
Our initial focus is enquiry-led education, training and wellness teams with repeated follow-ups. We assess fit during the walkthrough.

**Will WhatsApp, Instagram and website leads connect automatically?**
Not by default. Each connection requires a separate technical and permission review and is only included if explicitly verified and agreed.

**Does it send AI replies automatically?**
No autonomous messaging is included in the initial pilot. Human-controlled workflows remain the base scope.

**Can my team see every customer's information?**
Access is role- and organization-scoped. Real customer data is not activated until its required privacy and isolation checks are passed.

**What happens at the end of 60 days?**
We review what actually improved. Any continued paid service needs a new approved agreement; there is no automatic renewal.

**What if we are not ready to upload real data?**
We can discuss fit and review a safe demonstration first. We do not import sensitive customer records until the access and privacy gates permit it.
