# M-2026-10-19 — Event Intelligence deterministic predictions v1

The first Event Intelligence prediction layer is deterministic and evidence-bearing by design.

It ranks session conversion intent, page traffic decline risk, SEO growth opportunity, paid attribution/lead-quality signals, and bridges existing statistical lead-scoring outputs into the universal prediction ledger.

Every prediction records its feature state, model key/version/mode, reason codes, explanation, evidence, generation time, expiry and later outcome. Scores generated by rules are explicitly marked as uncalibrated; only outputs from the existing trained lead-scoring registry may be represented as statistical probabilities.

No prediction can autonomously change ad budgets, CRM stages, customer messaging, publishing or payments.
